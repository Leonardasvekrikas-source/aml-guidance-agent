## Where BM25 beats dense

These questions carry distinctive regulatory vocabulary that appears verbatim in the corpus. BM25 is very strong on rare exact tokens: a term with high inverse document frequency pins the passage almost by itself. An embedding places such a term near its semantic neighbours, which is exactly the wrong behaviour when the neighbours are other regulatory jargon that happens to be about something else.

## Where fusion loses to its better input

RRF has a real cost, and this is it. When one retriever is confidently right and the other is confidently wrong, fusion drags the correct passage down the ranking by promoting chunks the losing retriever ranked highly. Fusion buys robustness across question types at the price of the best case on any single one.
